# Customer Retention & Churn Analysis Dashboard
# Project Overview

This project analyzes customer retention and churn behavior using the Telco Customer Churn dataset. The objective is to identify factors contributing to customer attrition, understand customer lifetime patterns, and provide data-driven recommendations to improve retention and reduce churn.

The analysis was conducted using R Programming for data cleaning and feature engineering, while Power BI was used to create an interactive dashboard that supports business decision-making.

# Business Problem

Customer churn is a critical challenge for subscription-based businesses as it directly impacts revenue and customer lifetime value. Understanding why customers leave and identifying high-risk customer segments enables organizations to develop targeted retention strategies and improve customer loyalty.

# Project Objectives
Analyze customer churn and retention patterns.
Identify customer segments with the highest churn risk.
Examine the impact of contract types, internet services, and customer tenure on churn.
Evaluate customer lifetime value across different customer groups.
Provide actionable recommendations to improve customer retention.

# Key Insights
Customers on month-to-month contracts exhibit the highest churn rates.
Churn is highest during the first 12 months of the customer lifecycle.
Fiber optic customers show higher churn despite generating higher revenue.
Long-term contract customers demonstrate stronger retention.
High-risk customer segments represent significant retention opportunities.
# Recommendations
Encourage customers to migrate from month-to-month to long-term contracts.
Implement retention campaigns targeting new customers during their first year.
Bundle value-added services such as technical support and online security.
Monitor high-risk customer segments proactively.
Develop customer loyalty initiatives for high-value customers.
